# GPT on YouTube

[YouTube Summary with ChatGPT & Claude - Chrome Web Store (google.com)](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/youtube-summary-with-chat/nmmicjeknamkfloonkhhcjmomieiodli)
Using YouTube’s auto transcript, create a concise summary of the video. Structure the main points under five subtitles, with three detailed points for each section, to give a clear overview.

Here are three follow-up prompts to deepen analysis:
1. What examples or case studies are mentioned in the video?
   - Identify practical cases or scenarios that illustrate and support the main points discussed.
2. What are the possible criticisms or limitations of the ideas presented?
   - Highlight potential weaknesses or less obvious aspects that may need consideration.
3. How can these ideas be applied practically in my daily work/life?
   - Provide specific suggestions or a step-by-step action plan to implement the concepts discussed.

These prompts complement your analysis and ensure a richer, more practical understanding of the video content.
