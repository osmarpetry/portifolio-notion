### Flipped Classroom
#### Methodology
- Students study at home and complement in class.
- Tools: Khan Academy and reference videos (e.g., https://youtu.be/tSCN9nDNw_k).
- Tips:
  - Promote student protagonism.
  - Avoid overly complex lesson plans.
  - Use techniques like Odd One Out, KWL, and TBL.
