## Key takeaways (my phrasing)
- **Daily Notes → capture without criticism.** Dump ideas first; process later.  
- Use **`#Nota` only for notes outside the journal**, and move them to a curated folder (keep raw annotations elsewhere).  
- **Folders over tags** for organization (personal preference).  
- **Zotero + Obsidian (Zotero Integration plugin):** manage PDFs, send highlights/annotations into Obsidian.  
- Good companion videos/playlists:
  - *Writing with Zotero and Obsidian* (Danny Talks Tech)  
  - *Como usar o Obsidian para estudar* / “Everything starts with a note-taking system.”  
  - *How I simply avoid procrastination* (Josh Strife Says) — strong motivation clip.

## Workflow (adapted)
1. **Capture (Daily Note):** quick entries; no judgment.  
2. **Promote:** if it deserves to live outside the journal, tag **`#Nota`** and move to the **Notes/** folder.  
3. **Refine:** add source link, 1–3 atomic claims, and a next action.  
4. **Reading pipeline:** PDFs → **Zotero** → highlights → **Obsidian** (Reading Notes/).  
5. **Areas via folders:** `Areas/`, `Projects/`, `Notes/`, `Reading Notes/`, `Archive/`.  
6. **Mentoring angle:** show both my setup and how others (video) do it; include mymind/books cross-references.
